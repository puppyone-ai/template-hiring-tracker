# Product Designer: sample screening criteria

> Draft, requiring human confirmation before use. Evaluate role-related work evidence; do not automate a hiring decision.

## Observations

- Does the work show task flows and key states?
- Are design changes supported by evidence?
- Are personal responsibilities separated from team outcomes?

## Recording format

| Requirement | Evidence and source | Missing information | Interview verification question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| {{Requirement}} | {{Material location; otherwise No evidence seen}} | {{Gap}} | {{Question}} |

Missing materials do not prove missing ability. AI may suggest further materials and human review, but must not reject a candidate or change a human decision on that basis.
