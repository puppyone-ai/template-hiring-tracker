# Human and AI collaboration

| Work | AI assistance | Human confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intake | Check files, detect duplicates, extract facts, and cite sources | Identity conflicts and role mapping |
| Screening | Compare evidence with role criteria and identify gaps | Whether to interview |
| Interviews | Draft questions and organize notes | Evaluation, outcome, and next stage |
| Communication | Draft emails and todos | Recipient, content, timing, and actual sending |
| Offers | Check completeness and draft documents | Hiring decision, compensation, terms, and sending |
| Closure | Check records and links | Archive or confirmed hire |

Cite the source of each fact. Use Needs verification when evidence is unclear. Distinguish self-reported claims from verified facts, and label AI judgments `AI suggestion` or `Draft`.

Ask about role requirements and demonstrable work evidence. Do not add unrelated personal information or infer personal characteristics from a name, photograph, or background.

Fictional human decisions in this repository demonstrate fields only. They do not authorize any real hire, rejection, or communication.
