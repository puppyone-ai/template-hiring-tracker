# Rejection email: draft

Use only when a person has confirmed the decision not to proceed and explicitly authorized sending. Do not send based on an AI suggestion or add unverified reasons, evaluations, or future promises.

**Subject:** {{Company}} / {{Role}} application update

Hello {{Name}},

Thank you for taking the time to learn about {{Company}} and participate in the recruiting process for {{Role}}. After discussion, our team has decided not to proceed with this application.

We appreciate the experience you shared and the time you invested. We wish you well in your search and future work.

{{Recruiting contact}}
{{Company}}
