## General questions

1. Choose a relevant project and explain its goal, your responsibilities, collaboration boundaries, and outcome.
2. What was the main challenge? Which approaches did you consider, and how did you evaluate the tradeoffs?
3. What can you demonstrate or substantiate with materials?
4. How do you clarify an unclear requirement or resolve competing priorities?
5. Which tools do you use, and how do you verify AI or automation output?
6. What would you like to understand about the role and collaboration style?

## Role-specific prompts

- Design: explain information hierarchy, empty states, and error states, and how user feedback changed the design.
- Engineering: demonstrate a working flow and explain error handling, tests, and your contribution.
- Operations: explain an experiment's hypothesis, metric definitions, sample limitations, and retrospective.
